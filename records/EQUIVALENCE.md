# Equivalence with the research repository

This package was written for publication after the paper's results were produced, in the authors'
research repository. Every result here was regenerated by this package and compared with the original,
stage by stage, by a harness that ran the two code bases side by side (2026-10-25). Nothing was copied
from the original results except where a file is listed below as shipped unchanged.

## The records

`equivalence.json` lists every record, the original it replaces, how many values were compared and how
many differ. Every value is equal: 46,809 values across nine records. The only keys not compared are
provenance (when and where a record was made, file hashes and paths), plus two named exceptions:

- `source_sha256` of the deployed BiGRU ensemble: its weights now live in `models/bigru/pool_q16.npz`
  together with the other pool seeds, instead of in a five-seed file. The arrays are identical, and
  `random_letters/results.json`'s anchor shows the file reproduces the float checkpoints' quantization on
  all 1,250 transmissions of the first evaluation corpus.
- `determinism` in `random_letters/results.json`: the training-determinism check was re-run here
  (`training/determinism.json`).

`random_letters/oracle_conf8.json` replaces the research repository's pre-calibration record, whose model
rows equal the T = 1 block of `random_letters/results.json`; its oracle row is reproduced exactly.

## What is shipped unchanged

- the trained models (`models/`), the language-model table, and the BiGRU checkpoints;
- the firmware's C sources;
- the firmware goldens (`firmware/golden/`), the frozen test inputs (`data/golden_inputs/`), and the
  BiGRU training record (`training/bigru_pool.json`);
- the hardware measurements (`deployment/`), which cannot be regenerated without the bench.

## Not verified here

`deployment/silicon.json` carries the LIF board's worst-case stack (264 B) as reported at deployment.
`footprint_lif.txt` shows the frames it is made of, but the call chain behind that worst case was not
re-derived for this release.

## v1.1.0: the matched-data record (2026-11-08)

The paper's revision moved the comparison of record to matched training data (see the README). The
additions were gated against the research repository the same way (`tools/release/equiv/matched_compare.py`
there; `matched/equivalence.json` here).

| Stage | What was compared | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Protocols | the three new pre-registration hashes (`grid_bigru`, `grid_window`, `matched`), recomputed from `protocol/*.json`, and their chain back to the first two | equal |
| Models | the 40 feedforward models of the grid, the 24 BiGRU checkpoints of the three arms, the two deployed networks | byte-identical copies (sha256 against each model's training record) |
| Training corpora | the op12 and gen feature matrices regenerated by this package | sha256 equal to the recorded ones (seeds 0 and 9 of each; every retrained seed checks its own) |
| Training | LIF and MLP seed 0 retrained on the op12 and gen corpora | every pickle byte-identical (the op12 LIF is the deployed network); every BiGRU arm seed identical |
| | the BiGRU arms `rep`, `fist` and `shared` retrained on the GPU | every weight identical on all 8 seeds of each arm; the same five seeds selected in each |
| Posterior tables | every row of the grid on the eighteen corpora | consumed by the record comparison below |
| Record | `matched/results.json`, `fit.json` (every row's grid), `eval.json` (record and fresh seeds), `fidelity.json`, `criterion_1a.json` against the Exp 212 record and its Exp 210/211 inputs | every value equal: 196,966 values across the five files |
| Firmware goldens and headers | the deployed networks' posteriors and chain goldens, and `clf_weights.h` for both boards, regenerated from `models/*/op12/seed0.pkl` | every golden reproduced; both headers match |
| Firmware images | the two segmenter images built in both trees | 17 of 17 images byte-identical (the whole chain rebuilt) |
| Paper | the cited numbers and the five tables | numbers.tex and the five tables identical; the four figures pixel-identical |

Shipped unchanged in addition: the Exp 210/211 experiment records and the Exp 212 report
(`matched/research/`), the 2026-11-06 chain runs and the 2026-11-08 bench results (`deployment/`).

## v1.2.0: the spike-fed network (2026-11-11)

The paper's spiking network became the LIF fed spikes (see the README). The additions were gated against the
research repository the same way (`tools/release/equiv/run.py S3spk` and `spike_fed_compare.py` there;
`spike_fed/equivalence.json` here).

| Stage | What was compared | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Protocol | the spike-fed pre-registration hash (`spike_fed`), recomputed from `protocol/spike_fed.json`, and its chain to `grid_window` | equal |
| Models | the thirty models of the three arms (`models/{spk40,spk80,lif80}/op12/`), the deployed network among them | byte-identical copies (sha256 against each model's training record and the ruling's target) |
| Training corpora and forward | the op12 feature matrices regenerated by this package, and the training-side forward of every model against the inference forward | hashes equal to the recorded ones; every forward-check value equal (`spike_fed/check_train.json`) |
| Segmenters | every spike-fed row and the deployed network's Q16.16 row on the random-letter calibration corpus | 5.04 million values equal |
| Record | `spike_fed/results.json`, `check.json`, `fit.json`, `eval.json`, `criterion_1a.json`, `fidelity.json` against the Exp 213 record and port gate; `spikes_spk40.json` and `spikes_lif.json` against the spike-character records | every value equal: 63,497 values across nine files |
| Firmware goldens and headers | the spike-fed network's posterior and chain goldens (the chain golden's one beam death included) and `segmenter_spk/clf_weights.h`, regenerated from `models/spk40/op12/seed0.pkl` | every golden reproduced (339 of 339 window-net cases); the header matches |
| Firmware images | every image built in both trees (the status-LED table changed for the new module, so every image was rebuilt) | 20 of 20 byte-identical |
| Host gates | every board built for the host and run on its golden, the spike-fed module included | all pass |
| Paper | the 179 cited numbers, the six tables and the four figures | numbers.tex and the tables identical; the figures pixel-identical |

Shipped unchanged in addition: the ruling that adopted the network and the Exp 213 report (`spike_fed/research/`),
the 2026-11-10 chain run, bench result and footprint of the spike-fed module (`deployment/`).

`deployment/silicon.json` now also carries the spike-fed board's worst-case stack (376 B) as reported at
deployment; `footprint_spk.txt` shows the frames it is made of.
